# PROMPT 14 — SIH JUDGE REVIEW

Audit the current PRAGATI AI repo as a harsh SIH judge.

Do not code until after inspection unless a critical bug must be fixed.

Score 1–10:
- problem alignment
- core innovation
- AI usefulness
- technical depth
- feasibility
- data strategy
- demo clarity
- human-in-loop governance
- auditability
- scalability story

Then inspect the actual implementation and answer:
1. What is genuinely implemented?
2. What is mocked?
3. What is fallback-only?
4. What can fail during a live demo?
5. What would a judge challenge?
6. Is the matching engine truly doing semantic + contextual ranking?
7. Can the judge see source evidence and decision trace?
8. Can an incorrect AI match be stopped?
9. Does the dashboard update from the database?
10. Does the offline demo work?

Then give the top 10 fixes in priority order.

Do not praise features that are only planned.
